# Axiia Cup — Thoughts & Inspirations

## Game Conversation Design

- Allow direct conversation between player and any character (not just prompt-writing)
- Offer a **language transformer** (modern Chinese → ancient Chinese) to keep things in-character
- Design concept: the player is the **inner voice (内心OS)** of 商鞅 or 甘龙 — they hear, they guide, and the character speaks in their historical voice with the player's strategic input

## Core Game Design Principles

- **Feedback/prediction loop** is essential to make the game fun — players need to feel their choices matter and see consequences
- **Quick onboarding** — easy tutorial, bootstrap experience, good new-gamer guide
- **Start with PvE** before PvP; start with simpler scenarios first

## Progression Path (Easy → Hard)

1. Easy scenario (preset opponent)
2. Hard scenario (preset opponent)
3. Player talks to opponent directly (preset)
4. Player talks to own character, character responds in historical voice (preset)
5. Optionally talk to a harder opponent
6. Player writes a prompt for character to battle easy preset opponent
7. Player writes a prompt for character to battle hard preset opponent
8. Player can also try writing 甘龙's prompts — to test different 商鞅 strategies

## UX / Accessibility

- Need a way to show **minimal info and text** for players who don't like reading a lot
- Target: **100 words or less** to understand the final result of a match
- Design for skimmability — not everyone will read full transcripts
