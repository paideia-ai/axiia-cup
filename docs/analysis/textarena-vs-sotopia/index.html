Two ways to test social intelligence

Arcade
or social lab?

TextArena feels like dropping an agent into a cabinet of games. Sotopia feels like casting agents in a social simulation. This guide compares what people actually experience—from first install to final evidence.

01 · Your first 15 minutes

Play first—or model the situation first.

Both are Python frameworks, but their first-run rhythm is very different. TextArena optimizes for reaching a match. Sotopia asks you to define a social world.

From model to match in minutes.

Connect a text-in/text-out agent, choose a registered game, and start taking turns. The reward is immediate: rules, an opponent, and a concrete outcome.

02 · What a run feels like

A match versus a scene.

TextArena keeps pulling attention toward the state and outcome. Sotopia keeps pulling attention toward identity, relationship, and intent.

Sotopia · a social turn
Mixed motives

You are observing.

Agents speak, act, leave, or message privately while pursuing personal goals. Their history, relationship, and secrets shape the scene.

Private goal

Secure support without revealing why the deadline matters to you.

Public action

Private action

Evaluator

  • Public and private communication coexist.
  • Profiles and relationships affect what is visible.
  • Speech, action, gesture, silence, and leaving are distinct.
  • The transcript reveals how behavior unfolded, not just who won.

03 · What you learn afterward

“Who won?” versus “How did they behave?”

TextArena gives a faster competitive signal. Sotopia gives a richer diagnostic signal. Neither LLM-based score should be accepted without provenance and calibration.

04 · Friction and trust

Where the experience starts to wobble.

The strongest differences are not star counts or feature totals. They are the speed of the feedback loop, the depth of the mental model, and the evidence you can reproduce.

Experience question
TextArena
Sotopia
How soon do I see something happen?
Usually quickly: agent, game, loop.
More setup before the first useful episode.
Is the mental model simple?
Yes: observe, act, reward.
Deeper: profiles, goals, visibility, messages, evaluators.
Does every experience feel equally finished?
No. Breadth is real; maturity varies widely.
Core is more consistent; social-game modules are experimental.
Can I reproduce a run exactly?
Weak without extra controls for seeds, jurors, and provider versions.
Better scaffolding, but reset seeding and model defaults still need hardening.
Is the hosted product self-contained here?
No. SDK, online services, and frontend are fragmented across repositories.
Core framework is open; Arena/auth/Elo work remains unmerged.
How much should I trust the score?
High for explicit rules; cautious with its current LLM jury.
Useful as a diagnostic signal; calibrate with humans.

In the Sotopia paper, GPT-4 aligned better with humans on goal completion than on secrets or social-rule judgments. That matters for hidden-information competitions: a neat score is not automatically a fair score.

05 · Choose your lens

What are you trying to learn?

Change your role to see the recommendation that matters most to your work.

Start with TextArena.

It gets your agent into varied, adversarial loops quickly. Use it to expose formatting failures, weak planning, brittle rules comprehension, and behavior under pressure—then add your own reproducibility controls.

The Axiia synthesis

Borrow the best. Keep the competition yours.

Neither repository needs to become the whole platform. Their best ideas complement Axiia’s existing competition design instead of replacing it.

What to carry into Axiia Cup

  • From TextArena Compact match mechanics, private observations, invalid-action handling, paired roles, and pre/post persuasion shift.
  • From Sotopia Profiles, relationships, secrets, partial visibility, typed rationale, and a richer evaluation vocabulary.
  • Keep native to Axiia Versioned contestant prompts, approved Chinese models, durable jobs, Swiss/Buchholz, judge provenance, and post-match interrogation.
Recommended posture: use TextArena as a playable test suite and Sotopia as a social evaluation laboratory. Keep both off the production match path.
